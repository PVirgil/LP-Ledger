# 📄 LP Ledger

**LP Ledger** is your AI-powered assistant for private equity and venture capital fund operations. It transforms raw fund data into polished capital call notices, LP updates, fund metric reports, and legal summaries. Built for GPs, fund administrators, and LP-facing teams.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo

👉 [Launch LP Ledger](https://lp-ledger.streamlit.app)

---

## 🔍 Features

| Module             | Description |
|-------------------|-------------|
| 📨 Capital Call    | Generates detailed capital call notices from fund data |
| 📈 Fund Metrics    | Computes and explains IRR, MOIC, DPI, TVPI, etc. |
| 📝 LP Update       | Writes professional quarterly LP letters |
| 📁 Doc Summary     | Simplifies fund legal clauses in plain English |
| 💬 LP Q&A          | Simulates LP-style investor questions and responses |

## 💡 Why LP Ledger?

Fund reporting is time-consuming and high-stakes. LP Ledger automates key outputs with LLMs, saving time and ensuring clarity for your investors. From IRR analysis to email-ready LP updates, it's your co-pilot in fund operations.
